Water Treatment Systems

Purification, softening, and membrane technologies for high-purity industrial and commercial water supply.

Provisional Scope
Membrane

Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems

Engineered multi-stage crossflow filtration skids removing dissolved salts, minerals, and silica from brackish and borewell water.

  • Key Specs: High-rejection TFC membranes, stainless skids, automated flush
  • Applications: Boiler feed, pharmaceutical processing, drinking water facilities
Ion-Exchange

Water Softeners

Cation-exchange resin columns designed to exchange calcium and magnesium hardness ions with sodium, preventing scale deposition.

  • Key Specs: FRP vessel body, multiport valve (manual/auto), brine tank included
  • Applications: Commercial laundries, cooling towers, hotels, HVAC loops
Filtration

Iron Removal Systems

Catalytic oxidation and specialized manganese dioxide media filters designed to convert soluble ferrous iron into insoluble precipitates.

  • Key Specs: Birm/Manganese Greensand media, aeration injector unit
  • Applications: Borewell water with high ferric content, pre-treatment to RO
Deionization

Demineralization (DM) Plants

Dual-bed cation/anion exchange and mixed-bed polishers producing high-resistivity deionized water with virtually zero mineral content.

  • Key Specs: Degasification column, acid & alkali regeneration systems
  • Applications: Power generation, battery manufacturing, chemical laboratories
Membrane Barrier

Ultrafiltration (UF) Systems

Hollow-fiber semipermeable membranes with 0.01 to 0.05 micron pore ratings for total removal of suspended solids, colloidal silt, and bacteria.

  • Key Specs: PVDF hollow-fiber modules, automated backpulse sequence
  • Applications: Surface water treatment, tertiary RO feed pre-treatment

Wastewater Treatment Systems

Biological, chemical, and membrane bioreactor solutions meeting statutory discharge and recycling norms.

Provisional Scope
Sewage

Sewage Treatment Plants (STP)

Compact civil and modular packaged treatment plants utilizing biological aeration to treat domestic sewage for garden reuse and flushing.

  • Key Specs: Low energy consumption, quiet root blowers, tertiary filtration
  • Applications: Residential apartments, commercial complexes, educational campuses
Effluent

Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP)

Physico-chemical, coagulation, and biological treatment systems designed to neutralize trade effluents with high COD, BOD, and heavy metals.

  • Key Specs: Flash mixer, flocculator clarifier, automated pH dosing
  • Applications: Textile dye units, chemical processing, automotive and food industries
Biofilm

Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR)

High-density moving biofilm carriers suspended in aeration basins, providing immense surface area for microbial digestion within compact footprints.

  • Key Specs: Virgin HDPE media carriers, stainless retaining sieves
  • Applications: Space-constrained plants, capacity enhancement of existing STPs
Membrane Bioreactor

Membrane Bioreactor (MBR) Systems

Combines activated sludge biological digestion with submerged microfiltration membranes, eliminating the need for secondary clarifiers.

  • Key Specs: Flat-sheet or hollow-fiber membranes, high MLSS operation
  • Applications: High-standard reuse, cooling tower makeup, hotels and resorts
Solids Dewatering

Sludge Dewatering Equipment

Recessed plate filter presses and screw press dewaterers engineered to separate biological and chemical sludge into manageable dry cakes.

  • Key Specs: Hydraulic closing cylinders, polypropylene plates, drip trays
  • Applications: ETP sludge volume reduction, municipal treatment waste management
