Corporate Profile

Corporate Background & Core Activities

Headquartered in Ariyalur, Tamil Nadu, Chemsolv Associates operates with a dedicated focus on water treatment challenges faced by industrial, commercial, and municipal facilities. We identify suitable engineering solutions ranging from high-recovery brackish water Reverse Osmosis systems to biological Sewage Treatment Plants (STP) and Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP).

Our business activities center around:

  • Equipment & Spares Supply: Sourcing and distributing verified treatment components, filter media, dosing pumps, membranes, and instrumentation.
  • System Integration: Assisting clients in selecting the appropriate water purification or wastewater recycling configurations.
  • Regional Technical Assistance: Providing local technical advice and maintenance scoping within Tamil Nadu.

Domain Knowledge

Expertise & Capabilities

Understanding water chemistry, biological process demands, and mechanical filtration parameters is fundamental to our service delivery.

Water Quality Profiling

Evaluation of raw water parameters such as Total Dissolved Solids (TDS), Total Hardness, pH, Iron levels, and Suspended Solids to determine suitable pre-treatment and membrane configurations.

Process Instrumentation

Knowledgeable in specifying electronic flowmeters, pH controllers, and analytical monitors ensuring precise telemetry for water circuits and cooling loops.

Preventative Maintenance

Capability to structure comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for ongoing filter backwashing, membrane flushing, media top-ups, and pump maintenance.
