Our Services

Learn about the services offered by Chemsolv Associates and contact our team to discuss your specific requirements.

Service Scope Governance: The following services represent our proposed and verified service domains. Maintenance contracts, refurbishment schedules, and design scopes are customized after formal site parameters and raw water reports are evaluated.
Pre-Project Phase

Planning and Design

Request Design Consultation →

Thorough preliminary planning ensures that water and wastewater plants operate at peak efficiency with balanced lifecycle operating costs. Chemsolv Associates undertakes comprehensive assessments of inlet raw water quality, required treated effluent standards, hydraulic peak flow variations, and site spatial constraints.

Key Activities in Planning & Design:

  • Laboratory raw water testing & parameter profiling
  • Sizing of biological aeration tanks and clarifiers
  • Process flow diagrams (PFD) and hydraulic schematics
  • Chemical dosing calculations for coagulation & disinfection
Procurement & Logistics

Equipment Supply

Request Equipment Quotation →

We coordinate the sourcing and delivery of verified components, skids, and consumables directly to industrial and commercial project sites. Each item is matched to verified engineering specifications to ensure compatibility with existing infrastructure.

Supply Scope & Enquiry Workflow:

  • Sourcing of certified RO membranes (4040 / 8040)
  • Chemical dosing pumps, agitators, and multiport valves
  • Graded silica sand, activated carbon, and resins
  • Safe packaging and regional delivery to Ariyalur and nearby districts
Flagship Contract

Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

Request AMC Proposal →

Water treatment systems require rigorous routine supervision to avoid membrane scaling, biofouling, and sudden mechanical breakdown. Chemsolv Associates provides structured AMC agreements tailored to residential apartment complexes, commercial hotels, and manufacturing facilities across Ariyalur and Tamil Nadu.

AMC Agreement Coverage:

  • Scheduled monthly preventive maintenance visits
  • Regular testing of pH, TDS, DO, and hardness levels
  • Multiport valve servicing and backwash cycle verification
  • Emergency breakdown support and technical diagnosis
Restoration

Repair and Refurbishment

Schedule Inspection →

When equipment performance drops due to age or wear, full replacement is often unnecessary. Our refurbishment protocols restore pumps, control panels, vessels, and membrane banks to optimal operational capacity.

Refurbishment Capabilities:

  • High-pressure pump seal and bearing replacement
  • Chemical clean-in-place (CIP) membrane rejuvenation
  • Pressure sand filter and activated carbon media re-bedding
  • Multiport valve internal gasket and rotor repair
Modernization

Upgradation and Retrofitting

Enquire About Upgrades →

Existing treatment plants experiencing hydraulic overloads or tighter discharge criteria can be converted to advanced technology without rebuilding civil structures. We evaluate retrofitting options that maximize treatment output within existing tank footprints.

Modernization Solutions:

  • Converting conventional ASP systems to compact MBBR
  • Retrofitting coarse aeration systems with fine bubble diffusers
  • Automated level controls and energy-efficient VFD pump drives
  • Adding Ultrafiltration tertiary stages for wastewater reuse
Advisory

Technical Consultancy

Speak with an Advisor →

Navigating statutory environmental regulations and optimizing operational chemical consumption demands experienced technical oversight. Chemsolv Associates provides advisory support for industrial facilities and commercial infrastructure.

Advisory Scopes:

  • Environmental compliance alignment and discharge parameters
  • Water audit studies and internal recycling schemes
  • Chemical coagulant and polymer dosage optimization
  • Technical feasibility assessments for Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)
